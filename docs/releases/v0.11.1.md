## Scalpellino v0.11.1 — Cameras, step 2: Camera Preview and presets

**Camera Preview**
- A panel under Properties shows what the active camera sees, at the picture's shape:
  textures, the map's sky and fog, the rough light preview when it is on. Lens, near /
  far and orthographic cameras all show as they are set.
- It is drawn **only when you ask**: **Cmd+K**, its **Update** button, or World → Update
  Camera Preview. It doesn't follow every change, so the editor stays quick, and it tells
  you when the map has changed since.
- The panel is there only while the map has a camera.
- For now it shows the editor's look. With the renderer (next milestone) it becomes a
  close preview of the final picture.

**Camera presets**
- **Save preset** in Properties keeps a camera's lens, near / far, picture size,
  orthographic setting and exposure in the Bottega's `presets/camera_presets`.
- **Load a preset** puts them into the selected camera (one undo step), or into the next
  camera you place. Where the camera is and which way it looks stay as they are.

The right-hand panels have a new layout to make room for the preview: it is set up once,
the first time you start this version.

Milestone 11 (cameras) is complete. Next: Render, the Source look, with materials.
