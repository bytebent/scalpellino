## Scalpellino v0.10.6 — Sky Lab, step 3: night sky and effects

Sky Lab is complete: everything from Bryce's Sky Lab that belongs in a sky.

**Moon**
- Place it anywhere (it has its own compass); phase from new to full and back, with the
  lit side always toward the sun; size, brightness, glow, earthshine on the dark part,
  edge softness and colour.
- The built-in moon is NASA's colour map of the Moon (credit: NASA's Scientific
  Visualization Studio). Your own images go in the Bottega's `skies/moons/`: a 2:1 map is
  wrapped round the ball, a square image is used as the face.
- At night the moon lights the clouds and turns the sky a deeper blue.

**Eclipses**
- **Solar:** move the moon over the sun ("Move moon onto sun"). The sky darkens with how
  much is covered, the window shows the percentage, and a total eclipse shows the corona
  and the stars.
- **Lunar:** Earth's shadow moves across the moon and turns it copper red.

**Stars and comets**
- Earth's own sky (the stars you can see with the naked eye, from the Yale Bright Star
  Catalogue) or a random field, with intensity and amount, placed by latitude and star
  time. Stars show wherever the sky is dark.
- Comets with tails pointing away from the sun.

**Effects**
- Halo rings round the sun and the moon, with a secondary ring.
- A rainbow opposite the sun, with a secondary bow.
- **Fog in the sky:** the map's fog now has a base height and thickness (in Sky Lab and in
  World → Environment). Fog lying in a layer shows in the sky and over the ground.
- **Blend with Sun:** haze and fog take the colour and brightness of the sun's light.
- Volumetric World, colour perspective and lighting the scene with the sky (IBL) are
  already there and saved; they take effect with the renderer.

**Saved skies and Randomize**
- **Save sky** keeps the current sky under a name in the Bottega's `presets/skies`; load
  it into any map. **Randomize sky** makes a new one.

**Fixed**
- `.jpg` and `.tga` images never loaded: textures, skies and JPEG textures inside models
  came out empty. They load now.

Skies made with v0.10.4 and v0.10.5 look exactly as before.
