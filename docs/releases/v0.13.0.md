## Scalpellino v0.13.0 — water, clouds and endless planes

Milestone 13. Water as in Source, and Bryce's endless planes: water, ground and cloud layers
that run to the horizon.

**Water**
- A material with Source's **Water** shader makes the top face of a brush a water surface (a
  pool, a river, a lake inside the map). Imported Source water materials work as they are.
- Renders and the Camera Preview show **what the water reflects** and **what is under the
  surface**, mixed by Fresnel (more reflection at grazing angles) and rippled by a normal map
  (`$normalmap`, read through `$bumptransform`; a new built-in `water_ripple_normal` when the
  material names none).
- **Murky water:** what is under the surface fades into `$fogcolor` between `$fogstart` and
  `$fogend` (Hammer units), by how far you look through the water.
- **What it reflects, as in Source:** the map and the sky. Props and primitives show in the
  reflection only with `$reflectentities 1`.
- **Cheap water:** far away the water fades into cheap water: the reflection probes mixed with
  the murk's colour (`$cheapwaterstartdistance`, `$cheapwaterenddistance`). `$forcecheap 1`
  makes it cheap everywhere.
- Water and mirrors share the 8 reflection pictures per render (the ones covering most of the
  picture first); the rest is cheap water. The water surface must be level.
- Water lets light through in the bake.

**Endless planes**
- **World → Endless water plane / Endless ground plane / Endless cloud plane** (or **Endless
  plane with the current texture**): a flat layer at a height that runs to the horizon, with
  any material. Water planes work like water on brushes.
- Lit by the **sun and the sky only**: nothing casts a shadow on a plane (as in Bryce).
- In the bake a **ground plane blocks light** (an endless floor) and water lets it through.
- In the editor: a dashed line across the side views at the plane's height (the top view shows
  nothing, so planes don't cover the map), and a fading square of the material's picture around
  the map in the 3D view. Select a plane by clicking its line or its surface, or from **World →
  Endless planes**. Drag the line up or down in a side view, or type the height in
  **Properties** (also the material and the texture scale). **Delete**, **Cmd+D**.
- Saved in the map as our own entity, `scal_plane`.

**Clouds**
- A new **Clouds** shader for cloud planes: see-through cloud shapes made from noise, with
  cover, size, softness, detail, opacity and colours, lit by the sun from above and darker
  underneath where they're thick. They are separate from Sky Lab's clouds (which are painted
  into the sky picture).
- **Cloud shadows:** in the bake, the sun is dimmed by how thick the clouds are above each
  point, so the map lies in their shade.

**Starter materials**
- A new Bottega also gets `textures/starter/water` (clear blue-green, reflects everything),
  `murky_water` (brown), `ground` (grass) and `clouds`. In an older Bottega the three menu items
  write the one they need. Like the other starter materials, they may be removed in a later
  version.

**Differences from Source**
- Several water surfaces per picture (with the mirrors, up to 8) instead of one water height.
- No shoreline fade of the reflection where the water is very shallow.
- A camera under the water sees the surface from below without Source's underwater look (later).
