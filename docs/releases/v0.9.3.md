## Scalpellino v0.9.3 — move pieces, look around while replicating

**Pieces of a combination on their own**
- A click still selects the whole combination. **Option+click** a piece to select just
  it (in the 2D views its outline is enough, so a cutting piece inside the shape can be
  picked too). You can also pick a piece from the list in **Properties**.
- Once a piece is selected on its own, drag it, turn it with the rings or resize it with
  its handles: the rest of the combination stays where it is.

**Multi-Replicate no longer blocks the views**
- Its settings are now a floating window. While the preview is up you can pan, zoom,
  orbit and fly around to check the copies, then press **Return** to create them or
  **Esc** to cancel (from the window or from the views).

**Fix**
- Grabbing a primitive's size handle without moving it no longer snaps an off-grid size.
