## Scalpellino v0.12.7b — bending glass

Glass that bends what's behind it, as in Source: a material with Source's **Refract** shader.

- **What's behind the glass is bent by its normal map** (`$normalmap`, optionally
  `$normalmap2`). How far depends on `$refractamount`. It's tinted by `$refracttint` and
  optionally by a picture (`$refracttinttexture`).
- **Optional extras:** a little blur (`$bluramount 1`), a reflection on top (`$envmap`: the
  reflection probes or the sky, with tint, contrast and saturation), and
  `$fadeoutonsilhouette` (glass seen edge-on shows what's behind unbent).
- **How it works, as in Source:** just before the glass is drawn, the picture drawn so far is
  copied, and the glass shows that copy, bent. So it can only bend what's already in the
  picture: near the picture's edge the bending can smear, as in Half-Life 2. Glass further
  away is drawn first, so glass behind glass shows.
- **Light passes through** it in the bake.
- **Where it works:** renders and the Camera Preview. In the editor it shows its tool
  picture. On terrain and props it shows the reflection probes straight through.
- **Starter material:** a new Bottega also gets `textures/starter/glass_pane` (tinted rippled
  glass with a faint reflection). Like the other starter materials, it may be removed in a
  later version. Its ripples come from a new built-in normal map, `glass_ripple_normal`.
