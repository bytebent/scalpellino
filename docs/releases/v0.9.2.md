## Scalpellino v0.9.2 — centimetres, goopy shapes, landing and copying

**Units are now centimetres** (1 unit = 1 cm). The scale figure is 1.75 m (175 units),
and the default sizes for lights, fog, the terrain brush and stairs follow. Maps made
earlier keep their numbers; Transform → scale by 2.54 turns an inch-sized map into cm.

**Blending primitives** (Primitives, Properties)
- In a combination, give a piece a **blend** (in units) and its joint melts smoothly
  into the others: soft necks between spheres, soft rims around cuts, soft edges on
  intersections. 0 keeps it sharp.
- Colours and textures melt across the joint too.

**Snap to land** (Transform menu, **Cmd+J**)
- Selected primitives drop straight down until they rest on the brushes, terrain or
  primitives below (a combination lands as one; anything sunk in comes up).
- **Snap to ground…** puts their lowest point at a typed height.

**Multi-Replicate** (Edit menu, **Cmd+Shift+D**)
- Copies of the selection, each one step further: an offset per copy, plus a turn and a
  scale per copy for primitives (lights turn too). Brushes only move, so they stay on
  the grid. Turn about the selection, the world origin (rings) or a typed point.
- A live preview; **Return** creates them all as one undo step.

**Fixed:** Edit → Duplicate and Delete were greyed out when only lights or primitives
were selected.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Scalpellino-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Scalpellino.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Scalpellino.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
