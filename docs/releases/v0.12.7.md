## Scalpellino v0.12.7 — mirrors

Real mirrors, as in Source: a material with Source's **LightmappedReflective** shader turns a
flat brush face into a mirror, and renders draw what it reflects.

**Mirrors**
- Paint a mirror material on a brush face like any texture. Unlike Hammer, the brush doesn't
  need to be a special entity.
- Renders and the Camera Preview draw the scene again as seen in each mirror: lit, with props,
  primitives, decals, the sky and fog. The picture is as sharp as the render (half at Draft).
- Up to **8 mirror planes** per picture, the ones covering most of it first. Source draws only
  one. Any further ones, mirror materials on terrain or props, and a mirror seen inside a
  mirror reflect the reflection probes instead. The render log lists each mirror and warns
  when some were left out.

**Reflective glass**
- If the material also sees through (Source's `$refracttexture`, on unless you turn it off),
  what's behind the glass is drawn too. Reflection and see-through are mixed by Fresnel: more
  reflection at grazing angles, as in Source (`$fresnelpower`, `$minreflectivity`,
  `$maxreflectivity`).
- Tints for both, an optional normal map that ripples them, a mask, and an optional base
  texture lit by the baked light.
- Glass that sees through lets light through in the bake. A mirror that only reflects (a
  bathroom mirror, a polished floor) is solid to light.

**Starter materials**
- A new Bottega comes with `textures/starter/mirror` and `textures/starter/polished_stone`.
  They may be removed in a later version.
- A material without `$basetexture` shows its `%tooltexture` in the editor, as in Hammer.

**Differences from Source**
- Several mirrors per picture instead of one.
- The mirror picture is as sharp as the render. Source uses 1024 × 1024, stretched.

**Fixes** (a review of v0.12.6)
- Decals: equal-order decals keep their stacking after reopening; the order is kept within
  ±1,000,000; they're picked only where they show; their meshes are rebuilt only when their
  brushes change (much faster with many decals); the render push is capped at 1 cm.
- Material undo leaves a file changed outside the app alone, and says so.
- Snap to Ground works on combinations with cuts.
- A material can't be given a parent that would make a loop.
- Carve works when the cut leaves one smaller piece. Edit Curve is refused on a curve that was
  turned, mirrored, resized or reshaped since it was made.
- A prop model replaced under the same name is baked again; changing the fog rebuilds the
  cubemaps; shiny decals count for the "no probes" warning; probe sizes 1 × 1 and 2 × 2 say
  they're built at 4 × 4; Find Overlaps' results are cleared when you open a map.
