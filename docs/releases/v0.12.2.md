## Scalpellino v0.12.2 — Render, step 3: bounced light

The third step of milestone 12. Renders and the Camera Preview now **bounce the light**
the way Source's VRAD does (radiosity), so a sunbeam through a doorway lights the whole
room, ceilings aren't black, and colours bleed: a red floor tints the wall beside it.

**How it works**
- Surfaces are cut into **patches**: VRAD's default of 4 lightmap pixels, about 160 units
  at the default lightmap scale. Each patch's light is passed to every patch that can see
  it, again and again, until a bounce adds almost nothing (VRAD's rule), up to 100 bounces.
  The bounced light is then blended smoothly onto the lightmaps, so it stays soft like
  Source's.
- How much light a surface bounces, and in what colour, comes from its **texture's average
  colour**, capped at 99% as in VRAD.
- **Terrain** bounces and receives light. Painted layers count by how much they cover.
- **Primitives** gather the bounced light around them into their 3D lightmaps, so they
  are never darker than the walls beside them.
- **Primitives and props also reflect light** onto what's around them. Source doesn't do
  this; it was your choice. Props still aren't lit by the bake themselves (that comes
  with the light grid).
- nodraw faces still block light and bounce nothing.

**In the render window**
- After the luxels, the log shows the patches and transfers (with where the time went),
  then VRAD's own lines: `Bounce #1 added RGB(…)`, `Bounce #2 …`, until it settles.
- Draft and the Camera Preview stop at 10 bounces.

**World > Environment**
- **Bounce strength** (VRAD's -reflectivityScale): 1 = as Source, more for brighter bounced
  light, 0 for none.
