## Scalpellino v0.8.0 — world

**Lights**
- New **Light tool (Shift+L)**, also in the new **World** menu. Choose point, spot or
  sun and set its colour, brightness, reach and direction in Properties. Click in a view
  to place it (click again to adjust), **Return** creates it, **Esc** cancels.
- Lights show in every view in their own colour: a spot has a cone, a sun an arrow. A
  selected light shows how far it reaches.
- Click a light to select it (Select or Light tool). Drag it in a 2D view to move it.
  **Delete** removes, **Cmd+D** duplicates. Properties change every selected light.
- **Rotation rings:** a selected spot or sun gets rings to aim it. In the 3D view there
  are three (X red, Y green, Z blue); in each 2D view the one around the axis you look
  along. Drag a ring to turn the light in 15° steps, or type pitch and yaw.
- Saved as normal Hammer light entities, so other tools see them.

**Sky, ambient light, fog** (World → Environment)
- Three built-in skies (day, sunset, night). Add your own sky folders: 2:1 panoramas or
  4:3 cube crosses both work.
- Ambient light colour, and fog with a colour and two distances.

**A rough idea in the editor, like Hammer** (View menu, 3D view)
- **Sky** behind everything: on.
- **Rough light preview:** off. No shadows, and nothing is computed while it's off.
- **Fog:** off.
- **Scale figure:** a 72-unit person to judge sizes. Its spot can be typed (World →
  Scale figure position).

**Camera bookmarks** (World menu): save the 3D view's position with a name and jump
back to it. Kept in the map.

**Textures:** Grass_20 and Tile_13 are now built in.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Scalpellino-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Scalpellino.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Scalpellino.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
