## Scalpellino v0.1.1 — sharp, readable, menus

- **Sharp on Retina screens:** the app now draws at your display's full resolution.
- **New UI font** (Roboto) replacing the pixel font.
- **Fixed:** brush and preview edges that disappeared at some positions.
- **Menus for everything:** File, Edit, Tools, Transform, View, Help. Every shortcut is
  also a menu item; **Help → Controls** (F1) lists them all.
- **Transform dialog (Cmd+T):** move by an exact offset, rotate by any typed angle around
  X/Y/Z, scale or mirror; pivot at the selection centre, world origin or a typed point.
- Rotate 90° and flip commands, Duplicate (Cmd+D), Center 2D views on selection (Cmd+E).

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Scalpellino-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** `Scalpellino-apple-silicon.zip`

## First launch

Unzip to get `Scalpellino.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Scalpellino.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
