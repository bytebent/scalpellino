## Scalpellino v0.10.1 — Random Replicate

**Random Replicate** (from Bryce)
- **Edit → Random Replicate...** (**Cmd+Shift+R**) scatters copies of the selection around
  it. Each copy gets its own random offset, turn and size inside the ranges you type:
  offset up to ± so many units per axis, turn up to ± so many degrees per axis, size from /
  to a percentage (the same on all axes, or tick "each axis its own size").
- The copies show while you type and look around the views. **Return** creates them (one
  undo step), **Esc** cancels.
- **New seed** gives a different scatter; the same seed always gives the same copies, so
  you can go back to one you liked.
- Works on props and primitives (they move, turn and size), lights (move and turn) and
  brushes (they only move, by whole grid steps while snapping is on).
- The copies stay selected: press **Cmd+J** (Snap to land) to drop them onto the ground,
  terrain or whatever is below.
