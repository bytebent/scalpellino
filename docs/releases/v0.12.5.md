## Scalpellino v0.12.5 — Render, step 6: decals

Posters, cracks, stains and puddles on surfaces: Hammer's infodecal and info_overlay as one
object, the **decal**.

**Placing**
- **Decal tool** (**Shift+D**, or the new **Decals** menu): pick a picture in the Textures
  panel, click a surface in the 3D view. The decal shows there at its picture's size (texels
  × `$decalscale` × 1 Hammer unit, as Source sizes an infodecal), facing the surface. Drag
  to slide it, **Return** places it, **Esc** cancels.
- It lands on every surface in its box that faces it, brushes and terrain alike, and wraps
  over bevels and gentle slopes.
- It **belongs to the brush it was put on**: move, turn, size or copy the brush and the
  decal comes along; delete the brush and it goes too.

**Editing** (like props)
- Click a decal to select it (Cmd+click adds; rectangle-select in 3D takes them too); in
  the Decal tool, Option+click puts a new one over an existing one.
- In 3D: drag a **corner** to size it (Shift keeps its proportions), the **ring** to turn
  it (15° steps, Shift: freely), the decal itself to **slide** it over the surfaces.
- **Properties** or **Decals → Decal...**: position, width, height, turn, reach, order.
- **Order** when they overlap: the order they were placed, **Bring forward / Send back**,
  or type the number.
- **Delete**, undo, **View → Show decals**, and visgroups work on decals.

**Looks**
- Renders and the Camera Preview light a decal with the baked light of the surface under
  it (bump maps included); the bake itself ignores decals, as Source.
- **DecalModulate** (a new shader): darkens or lightens what is under it, for dirt, cracks
  and scorch marks.
- Full materials: a decal can have its own bump map, and `$envmap` for wet puddles.
- The editor's 3D view shows the picture flat on the surface; 2D views show its outline.
