## Scalpellino v0.12.6 — reflection probes

Shiny materials now reflect the room around them, as in Source: you place reflection probes
(Hammer's `env_cubemap`) and each render builds a small picture from each one.

**Placing probes**
- **Probe tool** (**Shift+E**, World and Tools menus): click a floor in the 3D view and the
  probe goes 170 cm above it; click a wall or a ceiling and it goes 81 cm out from it (32
  Hammer units). Or click a spot in a 2D view. **Return** creates it, **Esc** cancels.
- **Size** in Properties: Hammer's list, from 1 × 1 to 256 × 256 (default 32 × 32). Smaller
  means softer reflections.
- Probes are edited like lights: click the marker (a small ball), drag it in a 2D view or
  type its position, **Delete**, **Cmd+D**, the 3D rectangle select.
- Saved as Hammer's `env_cubemap` entities, so Hammer and TrenchBroom see them.

**Renders**
- A new step, **building cubemaps**: each probe draws the map around it (lit, with props,
  primitives, decals, the sky and fog), in linear light, so a lamp seen in a reflection stays
  brighter than white. The camera's exposure applies once, at the end.
- **Two passes** at Normal and High, so shiny things reflect each other; one at Draft.
- **Probe quality follows Render → Quality**: Draft halves each probe's size, Normal uses it
  as set, High doubles it (at most 512).
- The cubemaps stay in memory while nothing they show changes (moving the camera doesn't
  count). The Camera Preview builds Draft ones on Update.
- The log has one line per probe (where, its size, how long it took, how many shiny faces
  reflect it). It warns about a probe inside a brush, and about shiny surfaces in a map with
  no probes.

**Which surfaces reflect what**
- `$envmap env_cubemap` (Source's usual value) now reflects the **4 nearest probes, blended**.
  Source picks one probe per face; blending avoids jumps between faces, and a big terrain
  doesn't depend on a single probe. Probes behind a surface count much less, as in Source.
- Without probes, or with any other `$envmap` value (e.g. `$envmap sky`), it reflects the sky
  (cheap chrome), as before.
- Brushes, terrain, decals and props all reflect probes. Primitives show up in the probes but
  don't reflect them yet (they have no materials).

**Seeing probes in the editor** (View menu, both off by default)
- **3D view: reflection probes as mirror balls**: each probe as a small mirror ball showing
  its last built picture.
- **3D view: where the selected probe reflects**: select a probe and the shiny surfaces it
  shows on are tinted blue, stronger where it counts more.

**Differences from Source**
- Blending (above).
- Seamless filtering: the cube's edges never show. At 32 × 32, Source's DirectX 9 filtering
  can show them.
- No box (parallax) correction, as in Source 2013. Reflections on big floors "swim", as they
  do in Half-Life 2. Mirrors (the next step) will handle flat floors properly.
