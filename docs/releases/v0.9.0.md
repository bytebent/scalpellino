## Scalpellino v0.9.0 — math primitives

**Bryce-style shapes, perfectly round** (next to brushes; low-poly shapes stay as they are)
- New **Primitive tool (Shift+M)** and **Primitives** menu: sphere, cube (with rounded
  corners if you like), cylinder, cone, torus, capsule and disc.
- Pick the kind in Properties, drag a box in a 2D view (drag in a second view for the
  rest, or type the centre and size), **Return** creates, **Esc** cancels.
- They are drawn from their equations, so a sphere is perfectly round at any zoom, and
  they cut into brushes correctly. Textures wrap around them, or use a flat colour.
- The light preview and fog work on them too.

**Editing** (Select, Light or Primitive tool)
- Click to select, drag in a 2D view to move.
- Rings to turn them, like lights (15° steps).
- Size handles on each side: the opposite side stays put, **Shift** scales evenly.
- Everything can be typed in Properties: position, rotation, size, rounding, tube
  thickness, texture, colour, texture repeats.
- **Delete** and **Cmd+D** work; brush tools leave them alone.

**Combining, like Bryce**
- Select several and **Combine** (Primitives menu or Properties). Each piece adds,
  subtracts or intersects: cut a bite out of a cube with a sphere, or keep only where
  two shapes overlap. The cut shows the cutting shape's texture.
- Clicking a combination selects all of it; Properties lists the pieces to pick one.
  **Separate** takes them apart.

Saved maps keep primitives as primitives.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Scalpellino-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Scalpellino.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Scalpellino.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
