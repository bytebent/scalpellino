## Scalpellino v0.12.0 — Render, step 1: the pipeline

The first step of milestone 12 (Render, the Source look, with materials): you can render
pictures now. The lighting is still the rough one (lights and ambient, no shadows); baked
light and shadows come in the next step.

**Render**
- The **Render** button at the left end of the status bar, the new **Render** menu
  (Render...) or **Cmd+Shift+K** renders the active camera's picture, at the camera's size.
- A terminal window shows each step as it runs: what went into the picture (brushes,
  primitives, props, lights, sky, fog), missing textures or models, how long each step
  took. The editor waits while it renders; **Esc** stops it.
- The picture is saved in the Bottega's `renders` folder as
  `<map>_<camera>_<date-time>.png`, with the log next to it as a `.log` file. Without a
  Bottega, Render asks you to choose one first.
- A render shows the map as it is: the View menu doesn't change it (sky, fog, primitives,
  props, textured or flat), brushes you hid are in it, and nodraw faces are left out.
- Fog lying in valleys (Sky Lab's fog height) now shows on the scene in renders, not only
  in the sky. The editor's own fog stays the simple distance fog.

**Render options** (Render menu, remembered between runs)
- **Quality:** Draft, Normal, High. For now it sets the anti-aliasing (off, 2x, 3x); later
  it also sets the baked light and reflections.
- **Anti-aliasing:** off, 2x, 3x or 4x: the picture is drawn that many times larger and
  scaled down, for smooth edges. Very large pictures use less if the graphics card can't
  hold them (the log says so).
- **Texture filtering:** smooth (the default) or crisp pixels, for renders and the Camera
  Preview. The editor's own setting is still Texture > Filtering.

**Camera Preview**
- It is now a quick **draft render** (the same renderer, no anti-aliasing), so it matches
  what Render makes: it ignores the View menu and shows hidden things too.
- Update Camera Preview (Cmd+K) moved from the World menu to the Render menu.
