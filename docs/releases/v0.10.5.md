## Scalpellino v0.10.5 — Sky Lab, step 2: clouds

Sky Lab's **Clouds** tab adds Bryce's two kinds of cloud:

- **Stratus:** high, thin and wispy, drawn out in streaks.
- **Cumulus:** lower, thick and fluffy, with a lit side toward the sun and blue-grey
  shade, and a bright rim when you look toward the sun through them.
- Each layer has its own **cover** (how much sky), **frequency** (smaller formations as
  it rises), **amplitude** (from soft and faint to dense and hard-edged), **turbulence**
  (a stirred, denser pattern) and **complexity** (detail).
- **Cloud height** for both: higher clouds look smaller and further away, and widen the
  haze band at the horizon, as in Bryce.
- **Cumulus colour**, **spherical clouds** (rounder, denser clumps), and a **pattern seed**
  with a **New pattern** button for a different arrangement.
- Clouds take the light of the moment: white at midday, the sunset's colours when the
  sun is low, dark shapes at night (the moon will light them in the next step).
- **Clouds cast shadows** is already there and saved; it takes effect with the renderer.

Baking now uses all of your computer's cores, so skies with clouds bake quickly even at
8192 wide.
