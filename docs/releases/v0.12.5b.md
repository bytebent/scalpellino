## Scalpellino v0.12.5b — free turning with Shift

- **Hold Shift while dragging a rotation ring** to turn freely instead of in 15° steps:
  lights, cameras (the yellow roll ring too), primitives and props. Let go of Shift to go
  back to steps; the status bar says which. Brushes (Option+drag a corner) and decals
  already worked this way.
