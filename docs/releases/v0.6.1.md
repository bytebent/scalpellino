## Scalpellino v0.6.1 — fixes for domes, stairs and spheres

**No more shapes cutting into themselves**
- **Domes** with "round corners to whole units" on no longer have pieces that overlap.
  Rings now meet on flat horizontal cuts, and neighbouring pieces share the exact same
  side. A few corners can sit slightly off the whole-unit grid where four rounded points
  can't lie on one flat side; that's the price of a clean, overlap-free shell.
- **Spiral stairs with a centre column** no longer overlap the column on partial turns
  (e.g. 330°) or on several turns. The column's corners are now the steps' own inner
  corners. Over more than one turn, the steps per turn must divide a full circle evenly
  (e.g. 12 or 16 per 360°); otherwise you get a clear message instead of broken pieces.

**Much faster round shapes**
- Cylinders, cones and spheres are built directly from their faces. A 32-sided sphere
  now takes about 0.1 s instead of about 14 s.
- The block preview is built once per change, not four times per frame. Resizing a
  sphere with the handles no longer stalls.
- Vertex editing and snapping use a faster corner search: a 32-sided sphere rebuilds in
  about 0.1 s, down from about 28 s.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Scalpellino-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Scalpellino.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Scalpellino.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
