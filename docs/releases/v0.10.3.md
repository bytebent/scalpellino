## Scalpellino v0.10.3 — Faster with many props

Maps with hundreds or thousands of props are much quicker to work with:
- **Snap to land (Cmd+J)** among many props takes a fraction of a second instead of up to
  a minute: an object only looks at what is under it.
- **The 3D view** draws all props of one model together (one call to the graphics card
  instead of one per prop) and skips props that are out of sight.
- **The 2D views** skip whatever is outside them.

Nothing looks or works differently; it is just faster. Under the hood the editor's code
is reorganised into smaller parts, which makes the next features easier to build.
