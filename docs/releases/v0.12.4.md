## Scalpellino v0.12.4 — Render, step 5: materials

The fifth step of milestone 12. **Every texture is now a material**, the way Source does it:
a `.vmt` file beside the texture, in Source's own syntax and key names, says how the surface
looks in renders and the Camera Preview, which shader draws it, and how it takes part in
the light bake. The editor keeps showing the plain texture.

**The Material panel** (Texture tool → Properties → Material)
- Edits the materials selected in the Textures panel; picking faces selects their materials.
- **Shader:** Source's LightmappedGeneric (brushes and terrain), UnlitGeneric (no light) and
  VertexLitGeneric (props), shipped with the app as GLSL files; your own shader files in the
  Bottega's `shaders/` folder show up beside them (same inputs).
- A field for every setting of the shader. Bright keys are set by the material, dim ones
  come from its parent or the shader; **x** puts a setting back.
- Changes are written to a `.vmt` beside the texture (made on the first change), one undo
  step each (**Cmd+Z**). The map itself doesn't change. Built-in textures are read only:
  make a variant of them (it goes in the Bottega).
- Several materials selected: a change sets only that one setting on all of them; a
  setting that differs shows as "mixed".

**What materials can do in this version**
- **Glow** (`$selfillum`): the texture's alpha (or a `$selfillummask`) marks what glows.
- **Cheap chrome** (`$envmap env_cubemap`): reflects the map's sky, softened to about the
  size of Source's default cubemap, with `$envmapmask`, `$envmaptint`, `$envmapcontrast`,
  `$envmapsaturation`, `$basealphaenvmapmask` and Source's Fresnel (`$fresnelreflection`).
  Reflecting the nearest probe comes with the reflection probes.
- **Cutouts** (`$alphatest`, `$alphatestreference`): holes where the alpha is low, and the
  light bake casts cut-out shadows through them.
- **Cheap glass** (`$translucent`, `$alpha`): see-through, drawn last, far to near; it
  doesn't block light in the bake (as VRAD). Bending glass comes with mirrors.
- **Detail textures** (`$detail`, `$detailscale`, `$detailblendfactor`, `$detailtint`):
  Source's mod2x blend (mid grey changes nothing).
- **Texlights** (`%texlight "r g b brightness"`, lights.rad's format): the surface gives
  off light in the bake, one light per patch, with shadows, and it bounces.
- **`$reflectivity`**: how much light a material bounces, instead of the texture's average.
- `$color`, `$nocull`.

**Variants, parents, helper maps**
- **Make a variant...** (Material panel or Texture menu): a copy of one material's settings
  under a new name, the same picture. It starts identical and has its own entry in the
  Textures panel (marked **V**), so it goes on faces like a texture.
- **Parents** (Source's `patch`): **New parent from these...** puts the selected materials'
  shared settings in one parent `.vmt`; each keeps only its picture and what differs.
  **Edit the parent** changes all of them at once; the Parent list moves materials to
  another parent, or to none.
- **Helper maps** named `<texture>_normal`, `_ssbump`, `_mask` or `_selfillum` (and a few
  similar) hook onto their texture by themselves and are hidden from the Textures panel
  (tick **helper maps** to see them). The mask is used now; bump maps come next.

**Selecting textures**
- Click chooses one; **Cmd+click** adds or removes one; **Shift+click** selects a range.
- **Select all shown** after typing a search (by name or folder).
- **Texture → Select the textures of the picked faces**.
- How to select is written in the Textures panel too.

**Render menu**
- **Prop light: full / quick**: quick bakes props per vertex with a quarter of the rays (for
  maps with heavy props). The Camera Preview is always quick.

**In the render window**
- How many materials have settings, which shaders draw the picture, and warnings for a
  material that can't be read, a missing parent or texture, or a shader that doesn't
  compile (it falls back to LightmappedGeneric).
- From the bake: the surface lights, the cutout faces and the see-through faces.
