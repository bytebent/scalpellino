## Scalpellino v0.11.2 — Built-in skies, lighter skies

**Built-in Sky Lab skies**
- Sky Lab's **Load a saved sky** list now has 13 ready-made skies under **Built-in**, next
  to your own: Clear day, Sunset, Darker sunset, Painted dusk, Plain night, Clouds,
  Sunset clouds, Round clouds, Moonlit night, Comets, Solar eclipse, Lunar eclipse,
  Rainbow and halo.
- Load one, change what you like, then Apply or OK to bake it as usual. They work without
  a Bottega (baking still needs one). Your map's sun lock and baked size stay as they are.

**Lighter skies**
- The plain ground colour at the bottom of a sky is no longer kept in graphics memory:
  only the part down to a little past the end of the horizon's fade (5 degrees, to be
  sure). The rest is drawn in that colour, so it looks the same. A Sky Lab sky now uses
  about 40% less graphics memory.
- This works for any sky picture with a plain bottom. The saved PNG files are unchanged:
  still whole panoramas that other tools can use.
