## Scalpellino v0.11.0 — Cameras, step 1: camera objects

Cameras are objects in the map: the views Render and the Camera Preview will look through.

**Placing**
- **Camera tool (Shift+K**, World and Tools menus): set it up in Properties, click in a
  view to place it, Return creates it, Esc cancels. Cameras are named `camera 1`,
  `camera 2`...
- **One active camera:** the first camera of a map is the active one (a white dot in its
  marker). Tick **active** in Properties, World → Make selected camera active, or the dot
  in the camera tool's list to switch: only one is active at a time. Deleting the active
  one makes another one active.

**Turning and aiming**
- Click a camera's marker (a diamond) in any tool that selects; drag it in a 2D view.
- Three rings turn it (15° steps), a **yellow ring rolls** it around its view direction.
  Pitch, yaw and roll can be typed.
- **Aim at a point:** type the point in Properties, or World → Aim selected cameras at a
  clicked point, then click a surface in the 3D view or a spot in a 2D view (from the
  top view it aims level, at the camera's own height).

**Options** (Properties)
- **Lens:** field of view in degrees or lens length in mm (they convert), presets
  ultra-wide 14, wide 24, normal 50, portrait 85, telephoto 135 mm.
- **Near and far:** near cuts away what is closer, to look into a room through its wall.
- **Picture size:** presets (1280 x 720, 1920 x 1080, 3840 x 2160, square, portrait 4:5,
  3:2, 4:3) or any size typed.
- **Orthographic** (no perspective, isometric-style pictures) with its width.
- Marker colour; exposure (kept for the renderer's HDR).

Each camera is drawn as a pyramid toward its picture (a box when orthographic), with a
small triangle marking "up". Delete, Cmd+D and the 3D rectangle select work on cameras.
