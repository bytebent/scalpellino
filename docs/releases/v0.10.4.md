## Scalpellino v0.10.4 — Sky Lab, step 1

**World → Sky Lab…** makes your own sky instead of loading a picture, as in Bryce.

- **Sky modes:** Soft and Darker skies change colour with the sun (blue by day, glowing
  sunsets, dark at night). Custom uses three colours of your own (sky, sun glow, horizon),
  and Atmosphere off is one plain colour.
- **Sun:** drag it on the compass or type its azimuth and altitude. Also its colour, disk
  intensity, glow intensity, size, and horizon illusion (bigger near the horizon). Untick
  "sun visible" to hide the disk.
- **Atmosphere:** the haze band at the horizon (colour, density, thickness, base height)
  and a ground colour below the horizon.
- **Live preview:** while the window is open, the 3D view shows the new sky around your
  map, even with the View menu's sky switched off. A strip at the top of the window shows
  the whole sky at once.
- **Apply** bakes the sky into the Bottega's `skies/skylab/` (2048, 4096 or 8192 wide) and
  makes it the map's sky, as one undo step. **OK** applies and closes; **Cancel** (Esc)
  drops the changes. Sky Lab asks for a Bottega folder if none is set.
- **Sun lock:** with a sun light in the map, tick the lock and the sky's sun stays on it.
  Moving the sun in Sky Lab turns the light too. If you turn the light later, the sky is
  marked out of date: **World → Rebake Sky Lab sky** (or Rebake in the window) brings it
  back in line.
- The settings are saved in the map, so Sky Lab reopens with them. If the baked picture is
  missing (another computer, a new Bottega), it is made again when the map is opened.
- The scene-lighting settings (shadows, sun diffuse / specular, sky dome colour) are
  already there and saved; they take effect with the renderer.
