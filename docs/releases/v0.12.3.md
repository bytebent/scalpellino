## Scalpellino v0.12.3 — Render, step 4: lit props and the light grid

The fourth step of milestone 12. **Props are now lit by the bake**, like everything else in
renders and the Camera Preview, and the bake makes Source's **light grid**.

**Props**
- **Baked per vertex** (the default), as VRAD's static prop lighting: every vertex gets the
  direct light with shadows (its own too), the sky light, and the bounced light around it.
  Low-poly props get the slightly blotchy shading of later Source games.
- **Like a Source model** (Properties → lighting): the light grid's nearest ambient cube
  plus the 2 strongest lights it can see, shaded with Half-Lambert and no shadows on itself.
  The other lights are folded into its cube, as Source does. This is the classic soft,
  slightly floating Source prop look. Saved as Source's `disablevertexlighting`.
- A prop that doesn't cast shadows is still lit and still shadows itself.

**The light grid**
- Half-Life 2's grid of ambient cubes (light from 6 directions), one sample every 48 x 48 x 96
  Hammer units over the map, each from 162 rays: what the surfaces around it reflect, and
  the sky. Samples inside brushes and primitives are left out.
- **View → 3D view: light grid** shows the last bake's samples as small cubes, each side
  coloured by the light from that side.
- It is kept in memory like the lightmaps.

**In the render window**
- After the bounces: how many props were baked per vertex (and their vertices) or lit like
  Source models, and how many grid samples there are.
